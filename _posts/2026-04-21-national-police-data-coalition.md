The National Police Data Coalition was one of four Code for Boston projects I supported as a volunteer member during my Boston years. I joined in April 2023, when the project was being restarted.

## The project

The coalition's platform brings together independently collected police-conduct data from journalists, attorneys and community members, for researchers, lawyers, policymakers and the public. It started strongly in 2021, slowed in 2022 and was restarted in spring 2023.

## The tech

A Python Flask API with a PostgreSQL database and a Next.js front end. Volunteers ran the whole stack locally with Docker Compose, and the app was hosted on Heroku with a Heroku Postgres database.

## How I supported it

A restart brings a wave of new volunteers at once. I was on the non-technical side, guiding newcomers through Docker and account setup so they could run the platform on their own machine and contribute from their first evening.

## What I learned

**Restarts follow the same rules everywhere.** That same year, at work, I was restarting a programme that had been frozen for two years. The first blocker in both places was identical: people can't contribute until they can get in.

**Momentum is fragile.** A restarted project has goodwill but little patience. Quick, visible first wins for each new volunteer kept the energy up.

## Why it matters for my day job

Reviving stalled work is what I'm hired to do. This project confirmed the playbook: recover the context, make the first step easy and make progress visible quickly.
