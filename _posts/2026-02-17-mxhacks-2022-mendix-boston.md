I arrived in Boston in September 2022 to start on-site work for John Hancock. Within a few weeks I was at my first hackathon in the city: MxHacks 2022, "Low-Code for Good", run by Mendix, the low-code platform company that's part of Siemens.

## The event

MxHacks ran on 23 and 24 September 2022, with 36 hours of building. It was global: more than 1,200 developers from 64 countries took part, in person in Boston, London, Rotterdam and Singapore, or online. Teams of up to four built low-code apps for nonprofits chosen by Mendix, and more than 50 judges reviewed over 90 solutions.

Each region worked on a challenge for its own nonprofit. In the Americas, the partner was Project Alianza, which works to close the learning gap for children in rural Central America. The brief was a low-code app that would keep children learning at home or while they worked.

## What a delivery lead learns from low-code

**Speed changes the conversation.** Low-code puts a working screen in front of people within hours. Instead of debating requirements, teams react to something real. That's the fastest feedback loop I know, and it's the one I try to create in every sprint review.

**The hard part is still the problem.** When building gets easier, the real work moves to understanding the people the app is for and what will actually help them. Good delivery starts there, whatever the tool.

**Time boxes force honest scope.** Thirty-six hours leaves no room for "nice to have". Teams had to decide early what mattered most for the nonprofit and drop the rest, which is exactly the discipline that makes a release train predictable.

## The people

I was new to the city, and a hackathon is a fast way to stop being a stranger. I met people that weekend who became good friends, and we're still in touch today.

## Why it matters for my day job

I deliver large enterprise platforms, but the lesson from MxHacks applies everywhere: get something real in front of users early, keep the problem at the centre, and protect the scope that matters. It was a good first weekend in Boston.
