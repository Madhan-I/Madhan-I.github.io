I've spent more than twelve years running delivery for insurers, healthcare companies and retailers. Most of what I've learned never made it into a slide deck. It lived in sprint retros, steering committee prep and late calls with offshore teams.

This is where I'll write it down. Expect short, practical pieces on:

- Getting a stalled programme moving again
- Running an Agile Release Train that executives actually trust
- Putting AI into production in regulated environments
- Teaching teams so the practice survives after the trainer leaves

If any of this matches a problem you're working on, email me. I'm open to leadership roles and training engagements.
